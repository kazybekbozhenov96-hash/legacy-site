About the company

We understand the task first, then propose equipment

We work with mining and processing companies in Kazakhstan: open pits, processing plants, underground mines and bulk storage.

We specify equipment to your technical brief, equipment model or process parameters. We take into account the material, duty, climate and site constraints so the assembly works in real conditions, not just on paper.

We take on complete projects (conveyor design, turnkey crushing plants) as well as single assemblies, spare parts and consumables.
